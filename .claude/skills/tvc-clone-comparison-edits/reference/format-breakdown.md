## How the clones are made

Locked camera, plain wall. Each character is a separate take with the person standing in their own half (or third) of the frame; the takes are joined along straight vertical lines, which are invisible because the background is identical. Props and hands that reach toward the lens stay inside their own column. A doubled ceiling light in the first frames shows the layers are not perfectly aligned, and nobody notices.
