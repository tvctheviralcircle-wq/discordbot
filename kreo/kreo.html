How it works

Live in three steps

Sign up, then the setup guide walks you through each one. Your dashboard unlocks when you're done.

Step 1

Add your bot

Give your bot your brand's name and picture, then add it to your Discord server in two clicks.

Step 2

Pick your channels

Choose where each automation posts. New server? The AI Community Builder creates every channel with the right permissions.

Step 3

Connect your CRM

Paste an API key from Cruva, Euka or Reacher. Kreo reads your creators, videos and sales from there.

Two-way approvals

Approve in Discord or in Kreo. It posts once.

Every draft lands in your private mod channel and in your Kreo inbox at the same time. Whoever approves first sends it live. The other copy updates itself, so nothing posts twice.
